# Bold ACCESS Enrollment — Userbrain Test Script

**Prototype:** agebold.com/access sign-up flow (begins immediately after red flag screening)
**Persona:** Carol, 67, chronic pain, referred by her doctor to Bold

---

#### **Task #1**

Imagine you are Carol, a 67 year old woman with chronic pain. You've been referred by your doctor to use an online fitness-based, pain management program called Bold. You will now begin the sign up process.

As you interact with the prototype and continue through the sign up process, please carefully review each screen and think out loud your first impressions, areas of confusion or delight, anything that comes to mind.

To interact with the prototype:

- Please ensure you are using a laptop or desktop computer
- Restart the prototype by clicking the yellow "Restart prototype" button at the top right

⏭️ Continue to the next task.

---

#### **Task #2**

Continue through the screens where Bold asks about your pain and daily activity. You'll answer questions about where it hurts, how it affects things like walking or getting up from a chair, and how you like to exercise.

As before, think out loud as you go — what's clear, what's confusing, anything you're noticing about the questions being asked.

⏭️ Continue to the next task.

---

#### **Task #3**

Continue through the sign-up process. You'll now see a few consent and privacy screens before you can finish creating your account.

Please take a moment to actually review each one the way you normally would, and think out loud as you go. A few things we're curious about on these screens specifically:

- What's going through your mind as you read this?
- Is there anything here that would make you hesitate to continue, or anything you'd want to ask someone about first?
- Do you feel like you understand what you're agreeing to?
- Would you read this closely on your own, or skip ahead to check the box?

⏭️ Continue to the next task.

---

#### **Task #4**

Continue through the sign-up process to create your Bold account.

Think out loud as you fill this out. A few things we're curious about on this screen specifically:

- What are you thinking as you fill this in?
- Is anything about this screen confusing, frustrating, or slower than you'd expect?
- How do you feel about the password requirements?
- Is there anything that would make you want to stop here instead of finishing?

⏭️ Continue to the next task.

---

#### **Task #5**

Continue through the rest of the sign-up process until you reach your personalized plan.

Once you get there, take a look at what Bold has put together for you and share your first impressions — does this feel like a plan built for you? Is anything unclear or surprising?

This completes the prototype.

---

#### **Wrap-up questions** *(optional — add as a final "question" step in Userbrain, not a task)*

1. Thinking back on the whole sign-up process, was there any point where you felt like giving up or wanted to stop? What was happening on the screen at that moment?
2. Of the consent/legal screens you saw, was there one that stood out as confusing, too long, or off-putting? What would have made it easier?
3. What did you think about the account creation and password step?
4. On a scale of 1–5, how confident are you that Bold understands your specific pain and needs after going through this? Why?
5. Is there anything about this sign-up experience that surprised you, in a good or bad way?

---

### Notes for Monica (not part of the participant-facing script)

- The prototype's linear range is pain regions → plan results, matching what Task #1 already assumes.
- Task #3 covers three separate consent screens in sequence: a HIPAA privacy notice, a Medicare ACCESS program agreement, and a longer telemedicine/informed-consent + AI Scribe notice. Each requires checking a box before "Continue" unlocks — participants can't skip without at least scrolling to the checkbox, so listen for whether they actually read vs. immediately check-and-go.
- Task #4's account form pre-fills Carol's first/last name and only requires a password (8+ characters); the submit button is labeled "I agree" rather than something like "Create account," which may be worth watching for confusion in the recordings.
- Consider whether you want participant screening questions (age range, tech comfort, chronic pain history) added before Task #1 — Userbrain supports this as a separate screener step.
