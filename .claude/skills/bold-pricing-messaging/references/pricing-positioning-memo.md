78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket. You'll meet for a 45 minute virtual visit with a board-certified healthy aging provider. These visits are covered by insurance and our providers are in-network with various plans. Our team handles billing your insurance plan directly, and your personal Care Coordinator is on hand to answer any questions that come up for you.

Navigating insurance won't be your problem. Your Care Coordinator handles billing, answers your questions, and makes sure there are no surprises, so you can focus on your health, not your paperwork.

**Let's be clear about the numbers.**
When you sign up for a Bold healthy aging appointment, we will confirm that you are covered for a healthy aging appointment under your insurance plan. There will be no surprises — we'll clearly communicate your expected cost per appointment, based on your individual insurance plan. If you have any questions, your personal Care Coordinator is here to answer anything that comes up for you.

**Dive into the details:**
- 78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket.
- For the Bold patients who do pay a copay, the average out-of-pocket cost per appointment ranges from $5 – $55 — this depends on your insurance plan, your additional coverage, and other factors. You'll know a cost estimate for things like coinsurance, copays, and deductibles *before* you meet with a provider, and can choose to move forward (or not) as you see fit.
- For many insurance plans, Bold is a fully covered, $0 out-of-pocket cost. Other Bold members have supplemental coverage through Medigap, or secondary coverage through an employer, or a spouse. Supplemental and secondary coverage varies from member to member but often covers any remaining costs. Your Care Coordinator can help you answer any questions that you have about your cost estimate per appointment.
  - When you sign up, we'll review your insurance plan. For many members, we can confirm $0 out-of-pocket coverage right away.
  - For certain other plans (some Medicare Advantage plans, as well as members with supplemental and secondary insurance) your Care Coordinator will call to confirm your coverage details. Once we've confirmed your plan(s) over the phone, we'll provide you with a cost estimate for your appointment, and then confirm your visit with your Bold provider.

**What about coinsurance, copays, and deductibles?**
Bold Care directly bills your Medicare and/or other insurance plans. Since every member's insurance is unique, we'll understand your coverage before your appointment is confirmed. ## Internal Messaging Guidelines (rules — enforce these)

- **We never say "free."** Legally, it's not allowed. Don't use it.
- We say **"$0 out of pocket."** For messaging on the site we **always** contextualize that with the **"78% of Bold patients"** stat. Wherever possible the percentage of Bold patients paying out of pocket should be specific to the plan being advertised.
- **"Out of pocket" needs hyphens when it's modifying a noun**, like *out-of-pocket cost*.
- We say our appointments are **"covered by Medicare"** and **"in-network with Medicare plans and eligible Aetna, Anthem, Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare plans, and more."** When you need a shorter phrase (headlines, email subject lines, etc.) use **"appointments covered by Medicare."** For body text, help text, and places where more detail is better, use **"in-network with Medicare plans and eligible Aetna, Anthem, Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare, and more."**
- We say **"appointments are covered by Medicare"** or **"providers are in-network with United"** — **not** "Bold is covered."
- We won't get into the details on the website of what each plan pays — given that so many members have supplemental coverage, we don't want to confuse people who will end up most likely paying $0. We'll emphasize **"78% of members pay $0 out of pocket."**
- During the insurance review in the onboarding flow we *will* provide specific details on estimated cost for $0-confirmed plans. (Open question from memo: can we identify supplemental insurance during onboarding?)
- For everyone else, we'll communicate a price estimate over the phone via Care Coordinators who'll get the details on insurance plans and supplemental insurance. After clearly communicating the price estimate, we'll ask the patient if they want to move forward and confirm their appointment.

## Care Coordinator Messaging

**The two buckets — find out which group the member is in:**

*Original Medicare (Part A + B)*
- Member uses the red, white, and blue Medicare card
- No private insurer card (United, Humana, Anthem, etc.)
- Member costs are typically **coinsurance** (percentage based)

*Medicare Advantage (Part C)*
- Member uses a private insurance card (United, Humana, Anthem, etc.)
- Still Medicare, administered by a private insurer
- Member costs are typically **copays** (fixed dollar amounts)

**Then, find out if they have another supplemental insurance plan:**
- Do you only use this insurance card?
- Do you have another insurance plan through your employer, your spouse, or Medigap?

**Put all this information into pverify and give the member their cost estimate:**
- "It looks like this visit is covered! The cost estimate for your provider appointment is $X. Now that we know that, I can go ahead and get your visit confirmed."
