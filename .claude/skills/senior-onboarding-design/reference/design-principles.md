# Senior Onboarding — 6 Design Principles

Source: PrevMed design-principles board (PNG attached by Tzu-Yi on 2026-05-21). These six principles are non-negotiable. Every onboarding screen, flow, or copy block must be audited against them.

---

## 1. Design with empathy, not assumptions
*Inspired by: Ground your work in empathy*

We meet members where they are — in their health, confidence, and tech comfort. We never patronize, over-simplify, or assume literacy levels.

**What it looks like:**
- Clear, human language that informs without overwhelming
- Interfaces that adapt gracefully to diverse comfort levels
- Emotional as well as functional clarity

---

## 2. Exceed Expectations
*Inspired by: Embrace the Wiggles + Wow the People We Serve*

We create delight by giving more than what's expected — more clarity, more empathy, more thought in every detail. Small, meaningful moments of care build trust and joy over time.

**What it looks like:**
- Anticipate needs before they're voiced
- Craft the details — polish that shows pride and intention
- Surprise with warmth or encouragement, never gimmicks

---

## 3. Lead with Warmth and Humanity
*Inspired by: Ground Your Work in Empathy*

Our tone, visuals, and motion should feel human, safe, and kind, never clinical, sterile, or automated.

**What it looks like:**
- Friendly copy and warm imagery
- Avoid medical coldness; design for connection and belonging
- Every interaction feels like care, not compliance

---

## 4. Be Transparent and Earn Trust
*Inspired by: Own Your Numbers*

We respect our members' time, data, and autonomy. We show the "why" behind what we ask, and deliver on what we promise.

**What it looks like:**
- Explain how data improves the experience
- Show progress and results clearly
- No tricks, pressure, or hidden asks

---

## 5. Design for Clarity and Confidence
*Inspired by: Say the Thing You Think You Cannot Say*

We believe simplicity empowers. Our design should help members act decisively, not second-guess or feel at fault.

**What it looks like:**
- Step-by-step guidance that anticipates uncertainty
- Error-prevention and supportive recovery states
- Clear hierarchy and consistent patterns

---

## 6. Evolve Through Learning
*Inspired by: Think Again + Embrace the Wiggles*

Our design system, like our members, grows stronger through iteration. We test, measure, and improve continuously.

**What it looks like:**
- Release fast, learn faster
- Share findings openly across teams
- View imperfection as momentum, not failure
