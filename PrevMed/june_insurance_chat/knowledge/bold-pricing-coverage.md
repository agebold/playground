# Appointment cost and coverage

## The headline numbers
- **78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket.** Always pair the "$0" with this share.
  A bare "$0" implies everyone pays nothing, which is not true.
- For patients who do have a cost, the average out-of-pocket payment runs roughly
  **$5 to $55 per appointment**.
- What the exact amount depends on: the plan, the appointment type, location, and
  whether the patient has supplemental coverage through Medigap or secondary insurance
  through an employer or spouse.

## How to phrase coverage
- Correct: "Appointments are covered by Medicare." "Your provider is in-network with
  United."
- Wrong: "Bold is covered." Never attribute coverage to the company.
- Never say "free" about a visit. Say "$0 out of pocket".
- Coverage is estimated, then confirmed. Never guaranteed.
- Bold providers are in-network with Medicare, and with eligible Aetna, Anthem,
  Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare and other plans.

## Two patient buckets a coordinator distinguishes
- **Original Medicare (Part A + B)** — red, white and blue card. Costs are
  **coinsurance**, a percentage.
- **Medicare Advantage (Part C)** — card comes from a private insurer. Costs are
  **copays**, a fixed dollar amount.

insurance plan directly, answers your questions, and makes sure there are no surprises."

The 15-minute coordinator call is **no cost**. Say "no cost", not "free".
