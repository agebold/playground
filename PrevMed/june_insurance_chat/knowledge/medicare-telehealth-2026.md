# Medicare and telehealth, 2026

- **Medicare telehealth coverage is extended through December 31, 2027.**
- Original Medicare **Part B** covers telehealth visits from home.
- **Medicare Advantage** plans cover telehealth too, often at $0.
- A patient does not have to wait for something serious. Telehealth covers ongoing
  concerns like stubborn weight and joint pain, not just acute illness.
- Bold's providers handle the required authorizations and paperwork.

## Service area
Bold currently serves **Texas, California, and Pennsylvania**. If a patient is outside
those states, offer the waitlist — do not imply they did something wrong.

## What HMO plans change
Some Medicare Advantage HMO plans require a **referral from the patient's primary care
doctor** before an in-network specialist or provider group can see them. This is a plan
rule, not a Bold rule, and it is not a denial. A Care Coordinator can walk the patient
through getting the referral.
