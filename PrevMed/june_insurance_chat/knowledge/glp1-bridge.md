# Medicare GLP-1 Bridge

- Starting **July 1, 2026**, eligible members can get GLP-1s (Wegovy, Zepbound,
  Foundayo) for a flat **$50/month** through the **Medicare GLP-1 Bridge**, running
  through **December 31, 2027**.
- Bold's provider handles all authorizations.
- The $50 does **not** count toward a Part D deductible or out-of-pocket maximum.

## The rule that must never be broken
**Never say Part D — or any plan — automatically covers a GLP-1. It does not.**
Always frame access as "through the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge".

## Eligibility, in plain terms
Generally BMI 30 or higher, or BMI 27 or higher with a qualifying condition. A
diagnosis that routes through Part D (such as type 2 diabetes or moderate-to-severe
sleep apnea) sends the prescription down the standard Part D path instead of the
Bridge, where cost depends on the plan and remaining deductible.

Whether a medication is right for anyone is decided **with their provider**. Frame the
Bridge as "may be a good fit" — never diagnose, never promise.

## If a GLP-1 isn't covered
Stay open and honest. The member may still qualify through another condition, and there
can be other discounts depending on their plan. A Care Coordinator explores every option
at no cost before anything is prescribed. Lifestyle change is the foundation of safe,
lasting weight loss either way — that part is included and starts immediately.

## Keep the two prices separate
Appointment cost and medication cost are different models. Never blend them in one
sentence.
