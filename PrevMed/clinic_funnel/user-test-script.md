# Bold Care Clinic Funnel — Remote Self-Guided User Test Script

## Study Setup

**Format:** Remote, self-guided with think-aloud recording (e.g. UserTesting, Lookback, Maze)
**Duration:** ~30 minutes
**Device:** Mobile (iPhone preferred — prototype is mobile)
**Prototype:** Single URL — opens to Landing Page (Step 1), participants navigate all steps in order
**Participant criteria:** Adults 60+, Medicare-eligible, at least one health concern

---

## Research Goals

1. **Provider selection** — What do people consider when choosing a provider? What signals trust?
2. **Scheduling intent** — Do users want the earliest slot or their preferred time? What drives that choice?
3. **Map + Reassurance pages** — Do these screens increase confidence and clarity about the product?
4. **End-of-flow comprehension** — After completing the flow, do users understand what Bold Care is and what happens next?

---

## PROTOTYPE TASKS

*The prototype starts at the Landing Page. Participants tap through all steps in order.*

---

### STEP 1 — Landing Page

**Task shown on screen:**

> You're about to see the first screen of a health service app.
>
> **Before you tap anything** — read the page and answer out loud:
> 1. What is this service about?
> 2. Who do you think it's for?
> 3. Does anything make you want to keep going — or make you hesitate?
>
> When you've answered, **tap the main button** to continue.

---

### STEP 2 — Coverage Check

**Task shown on screen:**

> This screen shows insurance information.
>
> Read the page, then answer out loud:
> 1. What does this page tell you?
> 2. How does it feel to see your insurance already filled in?
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 3 — Eligibility Confirmation

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read this screen. Answer out loud:
> - In your own words, what did this just confirm?
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 4 — Journey Map ⭐ *(Key research moment — Goal 3)*

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read everything on this page **carefully** before moving on.
>
> Answer out loud:
> 1. What is this page showing you?
> 2. Does seeing these steps ahead of time make you feel **more confident, less confident, or no change** about what you're signing up for? Say why.
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 5 — Health Focus

**Task shown on screen:**

>
> Answer out loud: Why did you pick that one?
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

### STEP 6 — Your Health Concern

**Task shown on screen:**

>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

### STEP 7 — Health Intake

**Task shown on screen:**

> Fill out this form as you would if this were real.
>
> Answer out loud: Is there anything here you'd hesitate to share — and why?
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 8 — Reassurance Page ⭐ *(Key research moment — Goal 3)*

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read this page carefully.
>
> Answer out loud:
> 1. What did this page just tell you?
> 2. Did it change how you feel about this service — or not? Say why.
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 9 — Matching Animation

**Task shown on screen:**

> Watch this screen — no need to tap anything. It will move on its own.
>
> While you watch, answer out loud:
> - What do you think is happening here?
> - Does it feel real and meaningful — or more like a loading screen?

---

### STEP 10 — Meet Your Provider ⭐ *(Key research moment — Goal 1)*

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read the provider card. You can tap **"View profile"** to learn more.
>
> Take your time, then answer out loud:
> 1. What's your first reaction to this doctor?
> 2. What would make you feel confident — or not confident — about seeing them?
> 3. Is there anything you'd want to know that isn't shown here?
>
> When ready, **tap "Continue."**

---

### STEP 11 — Choose a Time ⭐ *(Key research moment — Goal 2)*

**Task shown on screen:**

> Browse the available times. **Pick the one you'd actually choose** if this were real.
>
> While browsing, answer out loud:
> 1. What are you looking for as you scroll?
> 2. Why did you pick that time — was it the earliest available, the most convenient, or something else?
> 3. How long would you be willing to wait to see the right doctor?
>
> Once you've chosen, **tap "Schedule appointment."**

---

### STEPS 12–14 — Privacy, Consent & Phone

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read this page and move through it as you normally would.
>
> Answer out loud: Is there anything here you'd hesitate to fill out — and why?
>
> Then **tap "Continue."**

*(Same instruction shown for each of the three screens)*

### STEP 15 — Confirmation ⭐ *(Key research moment — Goals 3 & 4)*

**Task shown on screen:**

> Read this entire page carefully. Take your time.
>
> When you're done, answer out loud:
> 1. In your own words — what did you just sign up for?
> 2. What do you think happens between now and your appointment?
> 3. How do you feel about the appointment being a Zoom video call?
> 4. Is there anything on this page that surprised you?
>
> You're almost done — no need to tap anything else.

## Researcher Analysis Guide

After all sessions, review recordings against four goals:

| Goal | What to listen for |
|------|-------------------|
| **Provider selection** | Which attributes are mentioned unprompted? What triggers trust? What feels missing? |
| **Scheduling intent** | Do they grab the earliest slot or scroll for the right time? How long are they willing to wait? |
| **Map + Reassurance pages** | Do participants say "confident," "clear," "reassured" after Steps 4 and 8 — or "didn't need it," "felt like filler"? |
| **End-of-flow comprehension** | Can they accurately describe the service after Step 15? What do they get wrong or miss? |
