Frequently asked questions

Can Bold Care help me lose weight?

Yes. Bold Care is a doctor-supervised weight-loss program built for adults 65+. Your provider creates a personalized care plan to help you lose fat while protecting the muscle, bone, and balance that matter as you age — with or without medication.

Does Bold Care prescribe GLP-1 medications? Which ones?

Yes — when it's the right fit, your Bold Care provider can prescribe FDA-approved GLP-1 medications, including pill options like Foundayo® and Wegovy® tablets, plus other options such as Zepbound®. We prescribe only FDA-approved medications — never compounded. Medication is one part of your plan, alongside the nutrition and strength support that keep your weight loss safe.

Do I qualify for the $50/month GLP-1?

The $50/month price comes through Medicare's new GLP-1 Bridge program. To qualify, you'll need a Medicare Part D (prescription drug) plan and to meet one of these health guidelines, which your Bold Care provider confirms during your visit:

  • A BMI of 35 or higher, or
  • A BMI of 30+ with a qualifying heart, blood pressure, or kidney condition, or
  • A BMI of 27+ with pre-diabetes or a history of heart attack, stroke, or peripheral artery disease.

Don't meet these? You may still be able to get a GLP-1 through your regular Part D plan — for example, if you have type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. Your cost would be different from the $50 Bridge price.

Not sure if you qualify? Check your coverage in a few minutes — we'll look at every option and confirm everything before your first appointment.

What will this cost me?

If you are prescribed a GLP-1, there are two costs associated with your appointment:

Your appointment:

  • Bold is in-network with Medicare and other insurance plans. 78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket. Bold bills Medicare directly, so you'll never get a surprise bill.
  • For many insurance plans, Bold is fully covered with a $0 copay. Other members have supplemental coverage (often through Medigap, an employer, or a spouse) that frequently covers any remaining copays. Your Care Coordinator can help you understand your cost estimate per appointment.
  • For the small percentage of patients who do have a payment responsibility, it's usually a modest copay for a virtual visit.
  • Depending on your plan, Bold healthy-aging services may count toward your yearly deductible.

Your GLP-1 medication, if prescribed — your cost depends on how Medicare covers it:

  • Through the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge: a $50 monthly copay, even before you meet your deductible.
  • Through your Part D plan (if you qualify another way, like type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea): your cost varies by plan. We confirm your exact cost with you, for free, before anything is prescribed — no hidden fees, no surprises.

We confirm your full coverage before your first appointment — no hidden fees. If you have any questions, please call or text us at (424) 577-5266. A Bold Care Coordinator is available to help you look up your specific plan details.

What if I don't want a GLP-1, or don't qualify?

That's completely fine. GLP-1s aren't right for everyone, and Bold helps you lose weight and build strength with or without medication. Your provider will build a plan around nutrition, movement, and accountability that fits your body and your goals.

Is it safe to lose weight at my age — will I lose muscle?

That's exactly what Bold is built to prevent. Rapid weight loss can cost older adults muscle and bone, so a GLP-1 alone isn't the plan — it's one piece. Your doctor-supervised program pairs it with protein guidance and senior-friendly strength and balance exercises so you lose fat, not strength. Your provider checks in on dose, side effects, and progress along the way.

What's included in my personalized Care Plan?

Your Care Plan is built with your Bold Care team and may include:

  • Medication management, if a GLP-1 is the right fit
  • Personalized, senior-friendly strength and balance exercise programming
  • Practical nutrition guidance — including eating out and prepared foods
  • Support for sleep, stress, and energy

No wearables, calorie counting, or meal logging — we meet you where you are. Your provider stays in the loop and adjusts the plan as you go.

Does Bold Care replace my regular doctor?

No — Bold complements your primary care. We help you stick with your weight plan between regular doctor visits, and you can share your progress with your PCP. You can also message your Bold provider about dosage, side effects, or daily habits and usually hear back within hours.

Who are Bold Care Providers?

Bold Care Providers are board-certified doctors, nurse practitioners, dietitians, and licensed social workers who specialize in healthy aging and supervise your weight loss and medication safely.

Is Bold available where I live?

Bold Care is currently available to members in California, Texas, Pennsylvania, and Illinois, with more states coming soon.

What else can Bold Care help with?

Beyond weight, Bold supports related conditions that often improve as you lose weight and get stronger — including joint pain and arthritis, high blood pressure, diabetes and pre-diabetes, heart health, and overall healthy aging.
