Per-tab detail

What each tab is for —
and what it isn't.

One compact card per tab. Read the anti-pattern line first; that's where the discipline lives.

🏠Home

One action at a time, progressive disclosure. Chat is the entry; one card is the focus; safety net is always one tap.

Primary surfaces

Today's one thing (default) with "Why this today?" reason · "Ask anything" chat (text + photo · routes meals/movement to AI, clinical questions to a real clinician) · Snapshot peeks (≤ 3, collapsed) · "Something doesn't feel right" safety-net button.

Won't doStreaks · badges · stacked dashboard · chatbot impersonating a clinician.
📈Progress

Body composition is the user's progress story; everything else sits underneath it.

Primary surfaces

Body composition module (default · weight, muscle, fat, bone density, fat distribution combined · inline scan CTA · "Scan fully clothed · Your data stays private") · GLP-1 dosage (Week X of 16 · Refill · Report a problem · Pause/hold) · Steps (weekly) · Protein-goal-reached · Food/sleep empty placeholder · Scan history · About DEXA-grade ⓘ.

Won't doStandalone weight card · scale-weight headline · prominent separate scan block · food/sleep without a linked device.
🧭Explore

A single browsing surface for workouts, recipes, restaurant guidance, and short articles — older-adult-filtered.

Primary surfaces

"For you today" personalized feed (default) · Workouts with filters (seated · balance-safe · joint-friendly · under-5-min standing) · Recipes matched to fridge / preferences · Restaurant & takeout guidance · Articles on GLP-1 + aging.

Won't doGeneric fitness imagery · food logging · stacked four-deep carousels · restrictive-diet framing.
👩‍⚕️My Care

Appointments and contact. Not the primary clinical chat (that lives on Home).

Primary surfaces

Upcoming appointments (default) · Past appointments · Schedule / reschedule · About my clinician · Contact (phone for urgent-but-non-emergency · 911 callout for emergencies, kept clearly separate).

Won't doCoach framing · daily check-in prompts · merging the 911 path with the routine-contact path.

Anti-pattern checklist Run before declaring done
Anti-pattern checklist

Run before declaring done.

If the IA violates one of these, it goes back — not the visual design.

  • Every screen ≤ 2 taps from a primary tab. No three-level navigation.
  • Primary nav is four tabs (Home → Progress → Explore → My Care). Profile is a top-nav button, not a tab.
  • Home contains one primary action card; never a stacked dashboard. Snapshot peeks collapsed by default and capped at three. principles #1, #2
  • "Why this today?" reason line is present on Home's primary action card.
  • Chat bar routes clinical questions to a real clinician with stated SLA — never a chatbot. principles #6
  • Photo input on chat is bounded — one-line nutrition notes + yes/no protein prompt. Never a calorie log, daily diary, or streak. findings #11
  • Progress leads with the body composition module integrating weight, muscle, fat, bone density, fat distribution. No standalone weight card or trend. principles #3
  • "Take a new scan" is a small inline CTA inside the body-comp module, not a separate prominent block.
  • Steps and protein are smaller cards under body-comp; never the page headline.
  • Food / sleep card is empty until a device is linked. Never prompts the user.
  • GLP-1 dosage uses "week X of Y" stepper, clinician-issued "what to expect," missed-dose guide, refill CTA, "Report a problem" safety-net CTA, and "Pause / hold dose" that auto-notifies the clinician.
  • No section is named or functions as a habit tracker, streak system, or daily logging surface. principles #1
  • Cost & coverage reachable in two taps (Profile → Medicare & coverage / Billing). principles #11
  • No tab or sub-page uses jargon ("metabolic," "telemetry," "insights"). Plain language only.
  • Notifications list the four allowed types and the forbidden types explicitly. principles #1, #6
  • DEXA-grade ⓘ explainer is reachable from Progress, not buried in marketing.
  • "Linked external devices" is opt-in, Profile-only, and produces zero notifications or nudges anywhere else.
  • "Share with my PCP" is opt-in, granular, shows last-shared timestamp, revocable at any time.
  • All three safety nets exist and are introduced during onboarding.
  • Marketing, onboarding, Home, and Explore stay device-agnostic — no wearable references outside the Profile opt-in.
  • Every journey stage has a non-empty IA home in the mapping.
Safety nets Three first-class IA citizens
Safety nets

Human support, transparent data,
easy escalation.

Older-adult digital weight-loss programs succeed when they pair the app with these three. None are buried; all surfaced in 1–2 taps; introduced once during onboarding.

01

"Something doesn't feel right"

One tap → pre-filled clinician escalation with topic picker (nausea · dizziness · pain · mood · something else). Bypasses normal SLA. A real clinician responds — usually within 15 minutes.

Lives on: Home (persistent button) · Progress → "Report a problem" · Help. Not a chatbot, not 911.

02

Trusted contacts (opt-in)

User names 1–2 contacts. Notified only on a transparent allow-list: missed scan + missed check-in for 30 days · clinician-flagged safety event · user-triggered "tell my contact I need help." Nothing else fires.

Lives on: Profile. Introduced during onboarding as optional.

03

"What Bold knows about me"

Quarterly plain-language summary: what data Bold collected, what the clinician saw, what was shared with the PCP, what changed in the plan. Any share is revocable here.

Lives on: Profile. Addresses this audience's anxiety about being talked-down-to with transparency, not just compliance.

Sources: Frontiers in Digital Health, 2022 · PMC11150599 · PMC12334334
