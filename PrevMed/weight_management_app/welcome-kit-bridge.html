A welcome from Bold's chief medical officer

A quick hello from Dr. Deep before you get started.

What is Bold Care's weight loss program?

Bold is doctor-led weight care made for adults 65 and up — working with your regular doctor, not around them. Your visits are with board-certified providers who focus on weight loss and healthy aging, all from home.

As we age, weight isn't just about the number on the scale. Your provider finds what's really driving it, prescribes a GLP-1 as part of your plan, and helps you lose fat, ease pain, and keep the weight off — so you stay strong and independent.

How Bold Care works

Three simple steps — and someone's with you at every one.

What is included in my doctor-supervised program?

  • Recipes and an exercise plan personalized to work for you

  • Side-effect monitoring and a prescription management tool in the Bold Care app

Who is your Care Coordinator?

Meet Maria — your dedicated guide

From your very first call, you'll have one person who answers your questions, sorts out the insurance, and keeps things moving with your provider. Your first call is a friendly 15 minutes — no cost, nothing to prepare, no pressure. Most people hang up relieved and genuinely excited to get started. You're never doing this alone.

How are we different from other GLP-1 sellers?

Lose weight the wrong way and you lose muscle — and your strength with it. The people we help most worry about two things: side effects, and the weight creeping back. Bold is built to prevent both.

Our program is grounded in science and led by board-certified doctors, so you lose fat, not strength — and stay steady, mobile, and independent.

We keep your regular doctor in the loop, so nothing slips through the cracks. And you'll never get a bottle of pills and a "good luck" — a real care team stays with you.

What it costs

Your Bold program runs through your insurance. Two simple costs — your visits and your medication — and you'll always know both up front.

78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket for visits, and your medication is a flat $50 a month — about $12 a week — for those who qualify for the Bridge. Your exact cost is on your dashboard, and your Care Coordinator is happy to walk it through with you.

Frequently asked questions

Is the first call a commitment?

Not at all. This first call is with your Care Coordinator — scheduling, and answering whatever's on your mind. It's no cost and no pressure, and you only book your provider visit when you're ready.

Does the provider prescribe the medication? What happens next?

Yes. When a GLP-1 fits your plan, your provider prescribes it — and builds a plan around your nutrition, movement, and daily routine to help you feel better while you lose weight.

Which GLP-1 medications can I get through Bold Care?

Only FDA-approved options — never compounded — including Wegovy and Zepbound. Compounded versions aren't FDA-approved and can carry added safety risks. Read more.

Is there an application I have to fill out for the Bridge?

No. Your provider confirms your eligibility at your visit, and your care team handles the rest behind the scenes. You just show up.

I took a GLP-1 before, through insurance or paying cash. Does that disqualify me?

No — a past GLP-1 doesn't disqualify you. Share how it went at your first visit, and your provider will fine-tune the dose and medication so it works better for you this time.

How do the GLP-1 options differ, and which is right for me?

They differ in how you take them — pill or injection — and in pace and side effects. Your provider matches one to your body and goals, and checks it against your other medications.

How do I join the appointment with my provider?

Your visits are on Zoom — you'll get a link by email, and your Care Coordinator can do a quick test call first so you feel ready. Joining on a computer? Keep your phone nearby, and your provider can call you if anything hiccups.

How does Bold work with my primary care doctor?

Bold works alongside your doctor, not around them. With your okay, we share your care plan so they're always in the loop — peace of mind, not another thing to juggle.
