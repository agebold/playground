What to expect in the appointment

You'll meet your Bold doctor by video. They'll review your health, answer your questions, and decide together whether a GLP-1 is a good fit — it's a conversation, not a commitment.

What is included in my personalized Care Plan?

A plan built around you: nutrition that's practical, movement that's doable at your pace, and — if it's right for you — medication with your provider staying in the loop.

Will I have any co-pay or co-insurance fees associated with my appointments?

78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket. We check your Medicare coverage before your visit so there are no surprises.

Does Bold Care offer primary care?

Bold Care focuses on preventive, healthy-aging care and coordinates with your primary doctor — we work with your PCP, not around them.

What happens at my follow-up visit?

You and Dr. Desai review how you're tolerating the medication and your measurements, then decide the next step together.

How to take it

Take one tablet each morning with a sip of water, at least 30 minutes before your first food, drink, or other pills. Try to take it at the same time each day.

What to expect

Starting your GLP-1

Your dose goes up slowly — "start low, go slow." Here's what most people feel, week by week. Everyone's different, and your provider adjusts your plan to you.

Weeks 1–4 · low starting doseYou're here

This is your body adjusting. Mild nausea is the most common feeling, and your appetite may drop. Eat small, frequent meals, skip greasy foods, and keep water nearby. Ginger tea helps many people.

Weeks 5–8 · first step up Check-in around week 3

Nausea can peak briefly after a dose increase, then settle. Around week 3 of each cycle, your provider reviews how you're doing and decides whether to move up, stay, or ease back. All three are normal — it's not going backward.

Weeks 9–12

For most people, nausea has eased and appetite settles at a lower level. This is a good time to lean into protein and gentle strength work so you lose fat while protecting muscle.

Months 3+

Steadier days. Some people notice constipation as a newer side effect — more fiber, fluids, and movement help. Your visits shift to every 1–2 months.

When to reach out
  • Nausea so bad you can't keep food or water down for a day
  • Vomiting more than twice in a day, or signs of dehydration
  • Severe stomach pain that spreads to your back — call 911

Missed a dose?

It happens — a missed dose won't undo your progress. Here's what to do with your daily Wegovy® tablet:

  • Missed today? Skip it and take your next tablet tomorrow at your usual time. Don't take two to catch up.
  • Missed several days in a row? Don't restart on your own — message your care team and they'll help you start again safely.
