Both personas — Tried-and-Burned (skeptical, has a bad GLP-1 history) and Curious-but-Wary (cautious, new to GLP-1s) — are confirmed adults age 65+, living in the US, Medicare-eligible. Every feature on this journey was designed against needs from both personas. If a feature only serves one, it doesn't belong here. The coverage matrix at the end proves both columns are filled.

Section 02 · Stages 0 – 7 End-to-End Journey · Both personas, every stage
The eight stages

From earning first trust to quarterly milestones.

00
Discover & earn first trust
VP #1 · VP #2
Trigger
"Why would this time be any different? Last GLP-1 cost me $400 to lose seven pounds."
"I want this to work — but is it safe for me? Is this even safe at my age?"
Feature
Trust-scaffolded hero. "Lose fat. Stay strong. Covered by Medicare." Pill is the default visual frame. FDA + 78% pay $0 + clinician-in-the-loop all visible above the fold. Acknowledgment line for past GLP-1 failure.
Action
See what's different, then click "See if Bold is right for me."
Won't doNo vial, syringe, or compounded medication. No "% body weight loss" headline. No "GLP-1 access" without cost/coverage attached.
01
Check coverage & form factor
VP #1 (full)
Trigger
"I don't want a surprise bill again."
"Will Medicare cover this? And do I have to take a shot?"
Feature
Coverage + form-factor preview. Real-dollar number ($50/mo · 78% pay $0) paired with a pill-first form-factor preview. Pen offered as secondary. Needle/vial deliberately absent.
Action
Enter Medicare info; see pill option pre-selected.
Won't doNever show "FDA-approved" without a coverage signal in the same view.
02
Schedule visit
VP #6
Trigger
"I need to know a real provider's involved this time — not a chatbot."
"I want a real clinician. Someone I can call when something feels off."
Feature
Schedule with a real clinician. Framed as "Your clinician will check whether a GLP-1 is right for you and stay with you through side effects and dosage." Safety-net language, never coach.
Action
Pick an appointment slot with a Bold clinician.
Won't doNo "Chat with your coach!" framing. No promise of daily check-ins.
03
Provider intake visit
VP #6 · VP #1
Trigger
"I want them to know what went wrong last time so it doesn't happen again."
"As you get older you have to worry about bone density. Will they take that seriously?"
Feature
Clinician intake with two acknowledgments. Captures prior GLP-1 history (P1) and age-specific priorities — bone density, strength, balance (P2). Clinician returns a starting plan, side-effect playbook, and body-comp baseline.
Action
Complete intake; agree on a starting plan.
Won't doNot a generic health questionnaire. No "metabolic health score" labs duplication.
04
Prescription & delivery
VP #1 (full)
Trigger
"I don't want the vial nightmare again. If I could do a pill, that'd be great."
"I'm definitely afraid of shots."
Feature
Pill-first prescription with no-surprise cost. Confirmation states the FDA-approved brand name, pill form factor, $50/mo (78% pay $0), and the side-effect playbook from intake. Welcome kit on the way.
Action
Confirm prescription; receive welcome kit and delivery date.
Won't doNo vial/syringe in confirmation, kit, or delivery email. No compounded medication.
05
Care plan reveal
VP #2 wraps VP #3 · #4 · #5
Trigger
"Just tell me what to do — I don't want to figure it out from scratch again."
"Don't make me figure it out from scratch."
Feature
Personalized care plan as the spine. One dashboard combining today's nutrition (5a), this week's strength & balance (5b), the body-comp baseline (5c), and the cadence of clinician check-ins (stage 6).
Action
Review the plan; see today's next step. Branches into 5a · 5b · 5c ⬇
Won't doNo daily-streak gamification. No habit checkboxes. The plan is guidance, not a tracker.
06
Chat with provider & check-ins
VP #6
Trigger
"Last time the nausea ended it for me — I quit at six weeks."
"What if something feels off? I want a person, not a bot."
Feature
Async patient-initiated chat with the clinician. Send a message about how they feel; log a clinical weight value shared with the clinician for safety (not the user's progress hero — that's 5c); upload an outside lab result. Stated SLA, no chatbot.
Action
Message clinician; log clinical weight; upload a lab if needed.
Won't doProvider as safety net, not coach. No daily prompts. No "Your provider is online!" framing. Weight here is clinical, not progress.
07
Ongoing care & milestones
VP #6 · VP #2 · VP #3
Trigger
"I want to know I'm protecting muscle and bone — and that cost stays stable."
"Is this still safe for me long-term? Will my clinician adjust the dose if I need it?"
Feature
Quarterly review + body-comp milestone. Clinician reviews body-comp trajectory (5c), side-effect history (stage 6), and adherence summaries (5a, 5b). Together they decide dose / taper / continue and renew the prescription. Milestones celebrate the kind of weight protected.
Action
Review milestone with clinician; renew prescription.
Won't doNo "you've lost X% of body weight!" milestone. No surprise renewal pricing. The $50/mo line holds.
Stage 05 branches into three parallel modules
05a · Nutrition
AI meal-prep assistant
VP #5 · "Don't make me log every bite"
Trigger
"I'm tired of restrictive diets. And I cook less than I used to."
"I want help eating well without logging every bite."
Feature
Conversational AI that talks to you before the meal, not after. Inputs: a photo of what's in the fridge (or a short text list), your mood/energy today, your stored preferences. Outputs: 1–3 recipe options tuned to dietary preferences, portion size, protein-forward nutrition goals, restrictions, and time available — with takeout/prepared-food fallbacks and on-demand menu reading when cooking is off the table.
Won't do"We don't log every bite." Photo is for personalization, not a food diary. No calorie counting. No restrictive-diet framing.
05b · Strength & Balance
Older-adult strength & balance plan
VP #4 · "Make the exercise feel doable"
Trigger
"Show me I'm protecting muscle, not just dropping scale weight."
"I can't stand for more than five or seven minutes."
Feature
Routines that read as accessible for a 65+ body. Seated by default, balance-safe, joint-friendly. Progress on a strength + bone-support axis. Standing tolerance assumed under 5 minutes. No equipment beyond what's already at home. Every illustration and demo is older-adult-specific, never generic-fitness.
Won't doNo generic-fitness imagery. No step counters. No wearable references. If it could be mistaken for a generic fitness app, redo it.
05c · Body Composition
DEXA-grade body composition ⓘ
VP #3 · "Show me progress that means something"
Trigger
"Last time I was just bouncing on the scale. I want to see I'm losing fat, not muscle."
"Body composition stands out more than the rest. Bone density matters as you age."
Feature
"DEXA-grade body composition · No clinic visit needed." Phone-camera scan once a month. Shows lean-mass preserved, fat-mass change, fat distribution, and bone-density indicators. Scale weight is never the hero — this is the user's progress story.
ⓘ  What "DEXA-grade" means DEXA is the gold-standard scan clinicians use to measure how much of your weight is fat, muscle, and bone. Bold uses your phone's camera to deliver the same level of insight — no clinic visit, no machine, no needles. Your clinician sees the same numbers they'd see from a hospital DEXA scan. Privacy callout Scan fully clothed · Your data stays private.
Won't doNo scale-weight headline. No wearable required. No before/after photos shown without explicit consent. No PCP-labs duplication.

Section 04 User Journey · Weight Management App
Anti-pattern checklist

Run this before declaring done.

Drawn from the research's anti-pattern list and "We're not" positioning. If the journey violates one of these on any surface, it gets sent back.

  • No needle, syringe, or vial imagery anywhere. principles #4
  • Pill-first appears in stage 1 visual treatment, not just stage 4.
  • FDA + Medicare / $50 mentioned together at least once in stage 0 or 1. principles #5
  • No "% body weight loss" language anywhere on the journey. findings #18
  • No meal logging, habit checkboxes, or wearable references in 5a / 5b / 5c. principles #1, #8
  • Provider stages (2, 3, 6, 7) use "safety net" language, never "coach" or "daily check-in." principles #6
  • Exercise references explicitly call out seated / balance / joint-friendly options. principles #7
  • AI meal-prep is framed as before-the-meal guidance, never as logging. Photo-of-fridge is input to recommendations, not a food diary. findings #10, #11
  • Phone-camera body-comp card carries the privacy callout "Scan fully clothed · Your data stays private" verbatim, under the feature description.
  • "DEXA-grade" claim has an ⓘ explainer next to it. Wording reviewed with the clinical team before external publishing.
  • Weight in the provider chat is framed as clinical safety data shared with the clinician — never as the user's progress hero. The user-facing progress story stays in 5c. principles #3
  • Provider chat is patient-initiated, asynchronous, with a stated SLA. No daily prompts, no chatbot, no "Chat with your coach!" framing. principles #6
