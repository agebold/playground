How it works

Your care follows five clear steps, and someone is with you the whole way.

Who is your Care Coordinator?

Your personal guide and advocate. Every member gets a dedicated Care Coordinator who walks you through each step, helps you get the most from the program, clears up insurance questions, and keeps things moving with your provider between visits. You're never navigating this alone.

Why this program works

Losing weight the wrong way costs you muscle. Bold pairs your medication with strength training and protein — so you keep your muscle and lose the fat.

Lose fat, not muscle.

What it costs

Two separate costs — your appointments and your medication. You'll always know both before you pay anything.

78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket for appointments — we bill Medicare directly, and you'll see your exact cost on your dashboard before your visit. Your GLP-1 is covered through your Medicare Part D plan, so it costs the same as if your own doctor prescribed it — no Bold markup. It's the real, FDA-approved medication billed to your insurance, never a compounded copy. Your plan sets the price, so your pharmacy or Part D plan can tell you the exact amount.

How to join the appointment

Your visits are on Zoom, right from home — on a phone, tablet, or computer. It's simple, and you can try it out beforehand. Your Care Coordinator can walk you through the setup on your call, and if anything goes wrong, we'll call you.

Why Bold is the right choice

Only Bold pairs an FDA-approved GLP-1 (covered through your Part D plan, at the same price as your own doctor) with a real nutrition-and-movement plan, side-effect tracking, and providers who stay in the loop — designed for adults 65+ to help you lose fat, not strength, and keep it off.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Is the first call a commitment?

No. This first call is with me, your Care Coordinator — my job is scheduling, logistics, and helping with anything that comes up. There's no cost for the call and no pressure. We'll go over what Bold is, answer your questions, and only book your provider visit if you'd like to move forward.

Eligibility & coverage

Am I eligible? Will Medicare cover it?

If you have a qualifying condition — like type 2 diabetes or moderate-to-severe sleep apnea — Medicare Part D can cover your GLP-1. You're in network with us, so your Care Coordinator confirms your coverage and you'll see your appointment cost on your dashboard.

Which of my health conditions could qualify me?

Medicare Part D already covers GLP-1s for certain conditions — most commonly type 2 diabetes and moderate-to-severe sleep apnea. If you have one, your provider can prescribe through your Part D plan, and your Care Coordinator confirms the path that fits you.

Which GLP-1 medications can I get?

FDA-approved GLP-1s that your Part D plan covers for your condition — your provider chooses the one that fits you best. We prescribe only FDA-approved medications billed to your insurance — never a compounded, cash-pay version.

Do I need a Medicare Part D drug plan?

Yes — this path covers your GLP-1 through your Medicare Part D (prescription drug) plan. If you're not sure whether you have it, your Care Coordinator checks with you and talks through your options. No guessing.

I've seen a $50/month price — can I get that?

That's the Medicare "GLP-1 Bridge" — a separate program for a different group, and it isn't part of Medicare Part D, so it won't apply here. That's okay: your GLP-1 is still covered through your Part D plan. And what makes Bold worth it was never a coupon — it's everything around the medication (a real care plan, side-effect support, and providers who stay with you) that your regular doctor usually can't give you.

Is there an application I have to fill out?

No application for you to fill out. Your provider confirms your coverage during your visit and works with Medicare to get your prescription covered — your care team handles the details.

I took a GLP-1 before, through insurance or paying cash. Does that disqualify me?

No. Taking a GLP-1 in the past doesn't disqualify you. Your provider reviews your history at your first visit and picks the right next step.

Do I need a certain BMI to qualify?

Not for this path — Part D coverage is based on a qualifying condition, like type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea, not your BMI. Your provider confirms what you qualify for.

Is my specific Medicare Advantage plan accepted?

Bold is in-network with Medicare and many Medicare Advantage plans. Your Care Coordinator checks your specific plan, and because Bold is a medical practice, we bill Medicare directly so you get the full benefit of your coverage.

Cost

What will it cost me out of pocket?

Two parts. Your appointments: 78% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket — we bill Medicare directly, and you'll see your exact cost on your dashboard before your visit. Your medication: your GLP-1 is covered through your Medicare Part D plan, so it costs the same as if your own doctor prescribed it — no Bold markup. Because your plan sets the price, your pharmacy or Part D plan can give you the exact amount. It's always the real, FDA-approved medication billed to your insurance — never a compounded copy.

Your visits

Does the provider prescribe the medication? What happens next?

Yes — if a GLP-1 is the right fit, your provider prescribes it. They also explore the parts of your routine that affect your weight — nutrition, movement, and your relationship with food — and build a personalized care plan. About a week later, your Care Coordinator checks in to see how you're doing.

How do the GLP-1 options differ, and which is right for me?

They differ in how you take them — pills or injections — and in side effects and pace. Your provider matches one to your body and goals, and explains it in plain language.

How does the Zoom visit work? Do I need to download anything?

Your visits are on Zoom — you'll get a link by email. Your Care Coordinator can do a quick test with you beforehand so you feel prepared (how to join the appointment is above). If you join on a computer, keep your phone nearby — if any Zoom issue comes up, your provider can call you to connect.

What if I'm not comfortable with technology?

That's completely okay. Your Care Coordinator can walk you through the setup and do a test call before your appointment, and if anything goes wrong on the day, we'll call you — you'll never be left stuck.

In person or virtual? Where's the office?

Everything is virtual, right from home — no travel, no waiting rooms. Being virtual also lets your provider see your home environment, which helps them build a plan that fits your daily life.

How often will I be seen?

You'll have your first visit, then your Care Coordinator checks in about a week later. From there it's every few weeks early on, easing to about every three months once you're steady.

Does Bold replace my primary doctor?

No — Bold complements your primary care. With your okay, we send a note to your doctor after your visit, so we're coordinating with your doctor, not going around them.
