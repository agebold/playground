## Weight Management Value Props (Clinic program)

> "We approach weight loss differently than other providers."

**Lose weight. Stay strong.**
Bold offers a provider-created personalized weight management program. Build strength and muscle while losing weight with easy lifestyle changes. Losing weight after 65 isn't the same as losing weight at 35 — metabolism, medications, joints all play important roles. Bold's physician-led care plans account for all of it.

**Covered by Medicare.**
Bold Care weight management appointment is covered by Medicare and other insurance plans. **86% of Bold patients pay $0 out of pocket.** <!-- Source record as written 2026-05-20. NOTE: this stat was corrected to 78% on 2026-06-26 per user; do not reuse 86% — see synthesis/findings.md #21 and .claude/skills/bold-pricing-messaging. Raw figure left intact to preserve the archive. -->

**A provider-created Care Plan with accountability baked in.**
Every Bold Care appointment ends with a personalized Care Plan — a document the provider makes just for the patient. Practical, easy-to-adopt habits across fitness, nutrition, and daily life. Because follow-up appointments with the Bold Provider are covered by insurance, the patient is held accountable for progress while working with someone who'll ensure they're losing weight *and* staying safe.

**Go beyond the scale.**
Lose weight to *do more*, not just to look different. Better balance, longer walking distances, waking up with less pain, more energy during the day. More than BMI — Bold works with the patient's PCP to track unique bloodwork panels that show progress.

**Exercise at the level that works for you.**
Fitness program tailored to health and lifestyle. Seated options, balance-safe routines, strength progression, osteoporosis/bone support, joint-friendly options. *"When your fitness routine is made just for you, it's easier to actually do."*

**Practical nutritional support.**
**Not** calorie counting or meal logging. Help the patient decide what to eat when tired and don't want to cook, ordering take-out, prepared foods, or scanning a menu. *"Restriction this is not."* Provider-led nutritional plan, no time-in-the-kitchen required.

**Support from experts in healthy aging.**
Matched with a Bold provider who specializes in the patient's goals and is an expert at working with older adults. Doctors, nurse practitioners, registered dietitians, and licensed social workers — all experts in helping seniors make real, lasting progress.

**Focus on lifestyle.**
> *"Bold doesn't default to prescribing you another medication, because our specialty is the 75% of your health that happens outside of prescriptions."*

High-impact lifestyle interventions — targeted nutrition and movement — that help medications work more effectively. Daily action plan that fills the gap between doctor visits.

## Weight Management User Needs + Associated Messaging

Six user needs paired with provider-voice copy. Use this as a copy bank for app screens, onboarding, and marketing.

### "Help me lose weight without getting weaker"

> Lose weight and stay strong with a weight loss Care Plan, made just for you by a healthy aging provider.
>
> Losing weight after 65 isn't the same as losing weight at 35. Your Bold provider builds a plan that accounts for your metabolism, your medications, your joints, and your lifestyle — so you lose fat, not strength.
>
> Bold's physician-led weight management program is designed to help you shed weight while building the muscle and balance you need to keep doing the things you love.

### "Don't make me figure it out from scratch"

> Your Bold Care Plan will include practical, small habits that will make moving and eating healthy easy to actually do. Your Provider will be available through messaging and followup appointments so that you can stay accountable, check in with someone who will keep you safe, and see real results that stick.
>
> Every Bold Care Plan is built by a provider who specializes in healthy aging, not a generic template. And because follow-up appointments are covered by insurance, you'll always have someone in your corner tracking your progress and adjusting the plan.
>
> Your Bold provider has helped seniors just like you make real, lasting progress. You'll get a step-by-step plan for fitness, nutrition, and daily habits — plus ongoing check-ins so you're never doing this alone.
>
> We fill the gap between your PCP visits with a daily action plan that actually fits your life.

### "Don't make me log every bite"

> Kiss calorie counting goodbye. We'll give you strategies to assemble low calorie meals that are packed with protein, fiber, and the nutrients you need to feel satisfied. We'll give you insight into eating at home, take-out, restaurants, and ready-to-eat meals so that you can be prepared no matter what.
>
> It's not about restriction — it's about strength. Your personalized Care Plan focuses on high-impact nutritional changes that help you lose weight and feel more capable, not less.
>
> This is not meal tracking. Your provider will help you decide what to eat when you're tired, ordering take-out, or scanning a menu: practical strategies that make healthy eating easy and satisfying.

### "Make the exercise feel doable for my body"

> No matter where you're at, physically, your Bold routine will feel doable yet impactful. Seated exercise routines, joint pain considerations, and modifications will all be included so that you can get moving in a way that works for you.
>
> Your Bold fitness program is tailored to where you are right now, with seated options, balance-safe routines, joint-friendly movements, and strength progression built in. When it's made for your body, it's easier to actually do.
>
> Whether you're managing joint pain, osteoporosis, or just getting back into movement, your routine will meet you where you are and build from there. So you can move safely, with your provider's guidance.
>
> We don't believe in one-size-fits-all fitness. Your program includes modifications and progressions designed specifically for your health profile, so every session feels challenging but never unsafe.

### "Show me progress that means something"

> We'll go beyond the scale to track progress over time. Whether that's waking up with more energy, going for longer walks, or just feeling happier in a body that is working for you again, we'll help you see meaningful improvement in your quality of life.
>
> It's not about how you look, it's about what you can do. Longer walks, less morning stiffness, more energy during the day. We'll help you see improvement in the things that actually matter to your quality of life.
>
> We'll think about more than your BMI. Your Bold provider will work with your PCP to track bloodwork panels and functional milestones that tell a fuller story of how your health is changing.
>
> Real progress is feeling good in your body again. We check in on the daily stuff — balance, energy, pain, endurance — so you can see that what you're doing is working, even before the scale moves.

### "Tell me why the clinician matters"

> Your Bold provider will assess how you're currently doing, propose areas of improvement within your Care Plan, and check in so you can see meaningful improvement. Working with a provider over time keeps you accountable, and helps you stay safe while losing weight.
>
> Our doctors, nurse practitioners, dietitians, and licensed social workers are experts in healthy aging. They've seen your challenges before and know what actually works for older adults.
>
> Losing weight safely after 65 means someone needs to be monitoring the full picture of your medications, your conditions, your energy. A Bold provider does exactly that.
>
> Because follow-up visits are covered by insurance, you get ongoing access to a provider who keeps you accountable, adjusts your plan as you progress, and makes sure you're losing weight without putting your health at risk.
