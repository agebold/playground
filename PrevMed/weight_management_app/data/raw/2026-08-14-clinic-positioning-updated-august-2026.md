## What our program is (p.6)

### Overview — a 12-month program in four phases

> A **12 month long** weight loss program that helps you build the foundation of healthy habits by focusing on "The Core Four" for weight loss, with or without medication.

| Phase | Window | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | First 3 months | Establish baselines and goals. Start with **a month of focused lifestyle shifts** (exercise, diet, stress, sleep, habits). Then a **shared decision on GLP-1 (Bridge, Part D, or none)**. |
| **Momentum** | 3–6 months | Muscle protection and medication dosage (if appropriate). **Deep-dive on sleep.** Full reassessment at 6 months. |
| **Resilience** | 6–9 months | Mood, identity, and social connection. If on medication, optimize dosage — **sometimes reducing**. |
| **Mastery** | 9–12 months | Review progress toward goals. Shared decision to **continue, taper, or graduate** from medication. Plan for maintaining weight, with or without medication. |

### The Core Four

- **Diet**
  - Protein targets based on weight (**1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight per day**), plus meal ideas for hitting the target.
  - *[For GLP-1s]* Check-ins to ensure adequate nutrition and protein while on medication.
- **Exercise**
  - **Resistance training classes 2× per week** in the Bold program
  - Additional **fall prevention** classes as needed
- **Sleep**
  - Ensure **7–9 hours** per night (with recommendations to get there if needed)
  - **Sleep apnea screening** and treatment plan **(?)** as needed *(question mark is in the source — unresolved)*
- **Stress and Mental Health**
  - **Mental health screening every appointment (PHQ)**
  - Connect to **group classes** for social connection and community
  - Available **licensed social worker** appointments as needed
