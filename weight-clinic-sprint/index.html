Sprint Context

Audience

  • Ages 65–85, Medicare-enrolled
  • BMI ≥25 or concerned about weight
  • 1–3 chronic conditions
  • Mobility/independence as primary motivation
  • MA PPO or Medigap (HMO excluded)

Research Method

Each prototype is shown to 5 participants during moderated research interviews. Reactions are compared across all three. The goal is to learn which positioning drives the most intent and confidence — not to launch any of these pages.

What We're Learning

  • Does strength & independence out-pull weight loss?
  • Does body-composition clarity feel reassuring or overwhelming?
  • Does "make it stick" land as honest or uncertain?
  • Where do GLP-1 questions surface spontaneously?

Important

Combinational method — the goal is not to pick a winner, but to learn which features, promises, and emotional frames participants would combine into their ideal version. Fictional brands only — no Bold brand, colors, or copy used.
