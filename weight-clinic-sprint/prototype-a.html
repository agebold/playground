What you get

A program that measures what actually matters at your age.

01 · Function

Stronger for daily life

Walk further. Carry more. Keep up with your grandchildren. We measure progress by what you can do — not by what you weigh.

02 · Body composition

Lose fat. Not strength.

Most programs focus on weight. We focus on lean muscle mass — the real predictor of how well you age and how independent you stay.

03 · Nutrition

Fuel strength, not restriction.

Protein-first eating with practical targets per meal. Meal ideas, grocery guidance, and recipes built around keeping you strong — not counting calories.

On GLP-1 medications

Use it right. Come off it safely.

Where clinically appropriate, GLP-1 medications can accelerate your progress — but only if you protect your muscle while losing weight. We build a plan that keeps you strong during treatment and gives you a clear path to taper off without regaining what you worked for.
